## Function

`/api/wcalert` is a fixed-scope, keyless proxy: no caller-supplied URLs, ids, or coordinates. Only `?src=` (a comma list of `nws,gauges,usgs,lake,quake,fire,air,spc`, or omitted / `all`) is accepted. Each source is fetched in parallel with its own 8.5 s deadline, TTL, and last-good fallback (flagged `stale`, never presented as fresh). A missing number stays null.

## What was not feasible / not included

- There is no live NWS or USGS gauge at Searcy itself. USGS 07076620 is retired (last IV 1987). Shown honestly; Judsonia is the live gauge for that Little Red reach.
- USGS 07076000 Little Red near Heber Springs also has no current data (last IV 1987) and is not listed as a live gauge.
- Nearby White River gauges on the card are Georgetown (in White County) and Augusta (just east, Woodruff County). Des Arc, Clarendon, and Calico Rock are further away and were left off to keep the card focused.
- County/zone NWS queries are used (ARC145 + ARZ033). Searcy County zones ARZ113 / ARZ213 / ARZ313 are a different county and are not treated as White County.
- EPA monitor AQI needs an API key, so the card uses a modeled Open-Meteo value and labels it as modeled, not a monitor.
- The existing US-wide ALERTS dock / NWS TX filter is unchanged; White County river-first coverage lives on the WHITE CO panel (the dedicated link), not as a third tab inside the TX-first alerts list.
